01Information you share

Globaxify, based in Bahawalpur, Pakistan, uses information relevant to enquiries and projects. This may include your name, email, phone, company and project details. Contact and newsletter forms prepare an email draft; we receive that information when you send the email.

02Why we use it

We use your information to answer enquiries, discuss or deliver agreed work, maintain business communications and operate the website. Review submissions and limited connection information may also be processed to display feedback and reduce spam. We do not sell personal information.

03Reviews are public

When you post a review, your name, optional company/city, rating, title and message are displayed publicly. Submitted reviews are stored by the review service. Please leave private or sensitive information out of your review.

04Service providers and links

Hosting and email providers may process information needed to operate their services. Some pages load Google advertising scripts and externally hosted fonts or icons; those providers may receive device or connection information. External websites follow their own policies. See our Cookies Policy for more.

05Storage and protection

We keep correspondence and project records while needed for the enquiry, service or applicable record-keeping obligations. Reviews remain until removed. Access is limited to relevant business purposes, but no online system can guarantee complete security.

06Your choices and requests

Ask us about information you have shared, request a correction or deletion, or opt out of marketing. Depending on your location, you may have additional rights, including access, restriction, objection, portability or a complaint to your local data-protection authority. Contact globaxify.official@gmail.com.
