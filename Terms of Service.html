01Website and project scope

These terms apply to using this website and engaging Globaxify. Services, deliverables, timelines, revisions and fees are confirmed in a written quote or project agreement. Agreed project-specific terms take priority where they differ.

02Your responsibilities

Provide accurate content, licensed assets, necessary access, timely approvals and a main contact. You remain responsible for the rights and legality of materials you supply. We may refuse unlawful, fraudulent, harmful or infringing work. Delayed inputs or changes in scope may affect delivery dates.

03Payments and refunds

Unless agreed otherwise: 50% is due before work begins, 25% at design approval/development completion and 25% before final delivery. Fees are in USD unless specified. Payment methods include bank transfer, cards, PayPal and Upwork.

Payments more than 7 days late may incur a 1.5% monthly fee; work may pause after 14 days. An advance is non-refundable once work starts. Other refund requests are assessed against completed work and circumstances, subject to applicable law.

04Ownership and licensed assets

You retain rights to your supplied materials. Custom deliverables transfer after full payment; our existing tools, libraries and methods remain ours. Third-party themes, apps and assets retain their own licence terms. We may show anonymized project examples unless an agreed NDA restricts this.

05Confidentiality and ending a project

Confidential project information is limited to relevant personnel; a mutual NDA can be agreed before sharing sensitive details. Confidentiality obligations continue for 3 years after services end.

You may end a project with 14 days’ written notice. We may end it immediately for non-payment, breach or unlawful conduct. Outstanding payments become due; completed work is delivered and any pro-rata refund is considered against work done.

06Liability and resolving concerns

To the extent permitted by law, liability is limited to the amount paid for the relevant project and excludes indirect or consequential losses. We are not responsible for third-party outages or delays beyond reasonable control.

Raise concerns with us first. If direct discussion fails, the existing terms provide for mutually agreed mediation, then binding ICC arbitration. Governing law and other project-specific arrangements should be confirmed in the signed project agreement. Nothing here overrides mandatory legal rights.
