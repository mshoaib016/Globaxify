Cookies Policy

A short guide to cookies, external services and browser controls.

Updated 18 November 2026•1 min read

01What cookies do

Cookies are small files stored by your browser. Similar technologies may also help services remember settings, maintain sessions or measure activity. Their purpose and lifetime depend on the provider.

02What this website uses

Public browsing and email enquiries do not require an account. Some pages load Google advertising scripts, which may use cookies or similar technologies for advertising and measurement. Externally hosted fonts and icons also involve requests to their providers. See how Google uses cookies for its purposes, durations and controls.

03Your cookie choices

Use your browser’s privacy settings to view, delete or block cookies, including third-party cookies. Google also provides advertising controls. Blocking a provider may affect its content or functionality. Simply reading this page or continuing to browse is not a cookie-consent choice.

04Questions and updates

We update this page when the website’s tools or practices change. For questions, email globaxify.official@gmail.com. Our Privacy Policy explains how other information you share is handled.
